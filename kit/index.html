Playbook — 7 steps

Ek time pe ek step. Har step ke end me ek action item hai — wo karke aage badho.

1

Niche pakdo (jo dabaav na kare)

"Sabke liye" matlab "kisi ke liye nahi". India me jo niche already paise de rahe hain: local businesses (gym, coaching, clinic, salon, PG, restaurant), real estate brokers, D2C sellers. Ye log already WhatsApp pe hain — wahan se milte hain.

  • 1 skill + 1 audience = niche. Jaise "WhatsApp automation for gyms"
  • Test: us niche ke 10 business ho sakte hain jo tuje 1 ghante me dundh sake?

Action: 3 niche likh, jo sabse jaldi 10 clients dikha de wahi kal se start.

2

Profiles setup — proof-first

Instagram bio me: kya karta hai + kis ke liye + kaise contact kare. Profile pe 3 posts pehle daal: (1) kaam ka screenshot, (2) process ya behind-the-scenes, (3) ek free tip. Bina posts ke DM bhejne se spam lagta hai.

Action: Bio + 3 posts ready kar. Post nahi hai to ek chhota demo project bana le aaj hi (apne liye).

3

Outreach — 10 roz, bina ruke

Math simple hai: 10 DM roz → hafte me 2-3 reply → mahine me 2-4 client. Haan, 100 me se 97 ignore karenge — ye normal hai, personal mat lena. Scripts section me ready-made messages hain, blanks bhar ke bhej.

Action: Aaj 10 business ki list bana (Instagram location tag, Google Maps, WhatsApp groups se). Kal se roz 10 DM.

4

Call — suno, mat becho

Call ka kaam samajhna hai, pitch nahi. 3 sawal sabse zyada kaam ke: "abhi ye kaam kaise hota hai?", "isme sabse zyada time/paisa kahan jaata hai?", "perfect ho to kaisa dikhega?". Jitna wo bole, utna hi bolna hai.

Action: Pehli call aane pe ye 3 sawal ratna, baaki sunna.

5

Close — number bol, phir chup

Proposal 1 page ka: problem → solution → timeline → price → terms. Price bolte waqt excuse nahi, hesitancy nahi. Bola aur ruk gaya — jo pehle bole wo hare.

Action: Templates section se proposal copy kar, apne pehle client ke liye bhar de — abhi hi, practice ke liye.

6

Deliver — chhota shuru, zyada deadline se pehle

Pehla project chhota rakh — 3-7 din ka. Deliverables WhatsApp pe likhit me confirm kar (kya milega, kab milega). Deadline se 1 din pehle de — referral ka foundation yahi hai.

Action: Scope document template bhar ke apne pehle project ke liye ready rakh.

7

Get paid + repeat

Rule: 50% advance, delivery pe baaki. Advance aane se pehle kaam shuru nahi — dost bhi ho. Har khush client se 2 cheezein: testimonial (sawal-prompt template Scripts me hai) aur referral ("kisi ko pata ho jhe ye chahiye").

Action: Payment terms ka message template copy kar, apne rate ke saath ready rakh.

Real Client Walkthrough

Ye humari (Beyond Pixells) asli journey hai — koi invented story nahi. Wahi cheezein jo is kit me sikhaayi gayi hain, humne khud ye sequence follow kiya.

1

Niche: gyms

Humne dekha ki local gyms Excel, registers aur WhatsApp pe chal rahe hain — member fees, renewals, follow-ups sab manual. Ye audience already paise de rahi thi (software nahi, par time aur lost renewals ke roop me).

2

Build: pehle demo, baad me polish

Ek working dashboard banaya (members, fees, follow-up reminders) aur landing page. Perfect nahi tha — real tha. Client ko dikhaya: "ye aapka kaam aisa ho sakta hai."

3

Pricing: setup + monthly

Humne flat pricing nahi liya — setup fee (ek baar) + monthly (recurring) liya. Yahi model is kit me calculator me hai: project income (setup) + recurring income (retainer) = stable agency.

4

Sale: demo ne bikri ki

Feature list ne nahi — chalta hua demo ne convince kiya. Isliye is kit me har jagah bola gaya hai: pehle demo banao (khud ke liye bhi), phir outreach. Proof pitch se bada hai.

5

Aage: ek client se portfolio

Ek real client ka kaam agle 10 clients ka pitch ban jata hai. Same playbook, ab proof ke saath. Aaj Gym OS live hai — gym-os-v3.vercel.app pe dekh sakte ho. Yahi sequence tu bhi chalayega.
