Playbook — 7 Steps

Ek time pe ek step. Har step ke end me ek action item hai — wo karke aage badho. Sequence todna = wahi galti jo 90% naye agencies karti hain.

1

Niche pakdo (jo dabaav na kare)

"Sabke liye" matlab "kisi ke liye nahi". India me jo niche already paise de rahe hain: local businesses (gym, coaching, clinic, salon, PG, restaurant), real estate brokers, D2C sellers. Ye log already WhatsApp pe hain — wahan se milte hain. Niches tab me 12 niche ka poora directory hai: pain, service, kahan mile, pehla pitch line.

  • 1 skill + 1 audience = niche. Jaise "WhatsApp automation for gyms"
  • Test: us niche ke 10 business ho sakte hain jo tuje 1 ghante me dhoondh sake?

Action: 3 niche likh, jo sabse jaldi 10 clients dikha de wahi kal se start.

2

Profiles setup — proof-first

Instagram bio me: kya karta hai + kis ke liye + kaise contact kare. Profile pe 3 posts pehle daal: (1) kaam ka screenshot, (2) process ya behind-the-scenes, (3) ek free tip. Bina posts ke DM bhejne se spam lagta hai.

Action: Bio + 3 posts ready kar. Post nahi hai to ek chhota demo project bana le aaj hi (apne liye) — ye tera proof hai jab tak client nahi mila.

3

Outreach — 10 roz, bina ruke

Math simple hai: 10 DM roz → hafte me 2-3 reply → mahine me 2-4 client. Haan, 100 me se 97 ignore karenge — ye normal hai, personal mat lena. Scripts tab me 25 ready-made messages hain: cold DM se lekar unpaid invoice tak.

Action: Aaj 10 business ki list bana (Instagram location tag, Google Maps, market walk). Kal se roz 10 DM.

4

Call — suno, mat becho

Call ka kaam samajhna hai, pitch nahi. 3 sawal sabse zyada kaam ke: "abhi ye kaam kaise hota hai?", "isme sabse zyada time/paisa kahan jaata hai?", "perfect ho to kaisa dikhega?". Jitna wo bole, utna hi bolna hai.

Action: Pehli call aane pe ye 3 sawal yaad rakh, baaki sun.

5

Close — number bol, phir chup

Proposal 1 page ka: problem → solution → timeline → price → terms. Price bolte waqt excuse nahi, hesitancy nahi. Bola aur ruk gaya — jo pehle bole wo hare. Objections tab me har "mehanga hai / budget nahi / soch kar batata hoon" ka jawab ready hai.

Action: Templates tab se proposal copy kar, apne pehle client ke liye bhar de — abhi hi, practice ke liye.

6

Deliver — chhota shuru, deadline se pehle

Pehla project chhota rakh — 3-7 din ka. Deliverables WhatsApp pe likhit me confirm kar (kya milega, kab milega). Deadline se 1 din pehle de — referral ka foundation yahi hai. Delivery tab me weekly update template, file naming system, aur handover checklist hai.

Action: Scope document template bhar ke apne pehle project ke liye ready rakh.

7

Get paid + repeat

Rule: 50% advance, delivery pe baaki. Advance aane se pehle kaam shuru nahi — dost bhi ho. Har khush client se 2 cheezein: testimonial (sawal-prompt template Scripts me hai) aur referral ("kisi ko pata ho jise ye chahiye"). Legal tab me advance, invoice, GST aur income tax ka honest basic hai.

Action: Payment terms ka message template copy kar, apne rate ke saath ready rakh.

Poora system ek line me: proof banao → roz 10 pahunchaao → sunno → number bolo → pehle de do → paisa + referral lo. Baaki sab shor hai.

File Naming Law

Ek hi system har file ke liye: YYYY-MM-DD_Client_Project_Item_vN — jaise 2026-10-03_PulseGym_Logo_Final_v2.png. "Final_final_2" naam wali file professional nahi lagti, aur 3 mahine baad kholo to khud ko bhi samajh nahi aayega.

Definition of Done (har project)

  • Deliverable client ke paas pahunch gaya (link/file), client ne dekha + samjha
  • Ek 5-min walkthrough (call ya screen recording) — "yahi hai, aise chalana hai"
  • Handover checklist complete: access, credentials, docs, backup
  • Final invoice bheja, payment aaya
  • Testimonial + referral request bheja (templates ready)

Is list ka last item log chhodte hain — wahi 2 cheezein (testimonial + referral) agla client laati hain.

Legal & Money Basics

Disclaimer: ye practical business basics hain, legal advice nahi. Pehle bade client ya milestone pe ek CA se 1 baar baat karle — ₹1-2K ka kaam hai, bahut bada darr khatam karta hai.

Advance — non-negotiable

50% advance, delivery pe baaki. WhatsApp pe likhit me scope + price confirm karna likhit record maana jaata hai — screenshot le ke rakho. Advance nahi de raha client = future me payment ke liye bhi nahi dega. Dost bhi ho, system same.

GST — kab chahiye

Services ke liye GST registration ₹20 lakh saal turnover pe mandatory hota hai (states me thoda alag). Naye agency wale ke liye: abhi chahiye nahi, par invoice khol ke sab record rakh — jaban (₹20L) paas aaye to CA se registration ek din me ho jati hai. Customer ko "GST bill" chahiye ho to bata dena — wo alag cheez hai, inter-party haisiyat.

Income Tax — bas itna

Freelance income tax ke hisaab se "business/profession income" hai. ITR file karna hoga (kaunsa form — CA se confirm). Saal bhar ka income-expense record ek sheet me rakh: invoice number, date, client, amount, expense. Ye 10 min/hafta ka kaam March me 10 ghante bachata hai. Expenses jo business ke liye hue (internet, phone, laptop) — proof rakho, claim hote hain.

Client Data — izzat + kaanoon

  • Client ka data (customer lists, contacts, business numbers) sirf uske kaam ke liye use karo — apne marketing me bina permission kabhi nahi
  • Kisi ek client ka data doosre client ko dikhana = reputation + kaanoon dono ka risk
  • India me DPDP Act hai — personal data handle karte ho to minimum ye rakhho: data kyun le rahe ho bol do, delete maange to delete karo, leak hone pe turant batao

Invoice Habits

Har payment ka invoice — chahe ₹500 ho. Invoice = proof + professional habit + tax record. Template Templates tab me hai. Unpaid invoice ka script Scripts tab me hai (#23) — 3 steps me polite-to-firm.
