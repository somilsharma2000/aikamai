# 30 Reel Scripts

Shooting notes: phone vertical, natural light, screen recordings in dark IDE theme. Face clips: talk like you're explaining to a friend, not presenting. ## Posting rhythm

- 1 reel/day, best slots 12-1pm or 7-9pm IST. Reels 1-10 week one, 11-20 week two, 21-30 week three+.
- Every reel: text hook on screen in frame 1, keyword CTA, reply to every comment within 1 hour (algorithm boosts early engagement).
- Reel 28 needs a real buyer + permission. Until then, keep it in the bank — never fake it.
- Reel 30's numbers: fill the real ones. Empty brackets = the reel doesn't go out.
