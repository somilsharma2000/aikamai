Privacy — the honest version

Last updated: 1 October 2026. A short, direct policy — because we genuinely keep very little data.

1. What the website collects

Nothing. The AI Kamai website has no login, no forms, no cookie banners and no third-party trackers. The pages are static.

2. What happens on WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, the conversation runs on WhatsApp (Meta) — their privacy policy applies to it. We use your name/number only to handle your order, and never sell it to anyone.

3. What we see in a payment

UPI payments happen inside your own banking app — we only see the payment confirmation and the amount. We never see or store your card/bank details.

4. What is saved on your device

The Vault/Kit web apps save your access key and your settings (favorites, profile) in your browser's localStorage — everything stays on YOUR device, never on ours.

5. Requesting your data

Under India's DPDP Act: if you want any of your WhatsApp records with us deleted, message us on WhatsApp — we will do it within 7 days.
